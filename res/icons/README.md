# Launcher icons

`pbstore.bmp` and `pbstore_f.bmp` are the menu icon and the focused menu icon of PB Store on the reader: 122 x 122 pixels, 8 bit
grey, like the icons of the sibling apps. They show a plain shopping bag with a plus sign, black on white (focused: white on
black). They are new artwork drawn by `scripts/make-icons.py` (Pillow), released under the project's MIT license; they contain no
PocketBook logo (constitution Principle VIII).

To draw them again: `python3 scripts/make-icons.py` (the files in this folder are replaced).
